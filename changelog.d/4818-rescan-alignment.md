- **A re-scanned part now lines up on its scan ([#4818](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4818)).** In the Android demo's 3D Scan, "Re-scan this part" refused almost every real re-scan. Two depth sessions of one room disagree next to each other's surfaces on a third to a half of their points, and the check that a re-scan does not contradict its scan counted every one of those. It now counts only surfaces put where the scan saw empty space. Fits a few degrees apart are read as one place, not two, and a part that holds the pose only weakly is refused instead, because on real re-scans those were the ones that came back wrong.
