- **A re-scanned part now lines up on its scan ([#4818](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4818)).** In the Android demo's 3D Scan, "Re-scan this part" refused almost every real re-scan. Two depth sessions of one room disagree next to each other's surfaces on a third to a half of their points, and the check that a re-scan does not contradict its scan counted every one of those. A re-scan is now refused as contradicting when more than a quarter of it, outside the parts to fill in, lands where the scan saw empty space, or when its walls sit beside the scan's without matching them, as a neighbouring room's do: more than three quarters of them off the scan's surface, or more than 40% of them 8 to 12 cm from it. Two fits within 10° and 10 cm of each other are read as one place, not two; a re-scan with fewer than 300 samples outside the parts to fill in is refused as overlapping too little, and one that holds the pose only weakly is refused, because on real re-scans those were the ones that came back wrong.
