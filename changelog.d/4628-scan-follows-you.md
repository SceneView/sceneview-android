- **Demo app: 3D Scan's live view follows you like a navigation app ([#4628](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4628)).** While you scan, the coloured point cloud now fills the screen over a dimmed camera, so what is scanned shows in its real colours and what is still missing shows on the camera behind it. The view stands just behind and above the phone and turns with it, heading-up: the way you face is always the top of the screen, and a marker shows where you are. Drag or pinch to look around, and Recenter brings the view back on you. The big 3D card that covered the top half of the screen is gone.
