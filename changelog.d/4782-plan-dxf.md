- **Demo app: the 3D Scan floor plan can be shared as a DXF drawing ([#4782](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4782)).** A "Share DXF (CAD)" action right after "Share PDF" hands over the plan alone as a DXF AC1015 (R2000) file in millimetres, typed `application/dxf`. Walls, openings, dimensions, the room's name and area and a "verify on site" legend each sit on their own layer.
