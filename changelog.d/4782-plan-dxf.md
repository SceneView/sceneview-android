- **Demo app: the 3D Scan floor plan can be shared as a DXF drawing ([#4782](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4782)).** A "Share DXF (CAD)" action sits right after "Share PDF" in the plan's share menu and hands over the drawing alone, typed `application/dxf`; "Share PDF" still hands over the PDF alone, typed `application/pdf`. The drawing is AutoCAD 2000 (AC1015) ASCII in millimetres, with the walls as one closed polyline and the wall lengths, the room's name and area and a "verify on site" legend on their own layers (`WALLS`, `OPENINGS`, `DIMENSIONS`, `TEXT`, `LEGEND`). Dimensions are plain lines and text carrying the plan's own figures and marks. The writer is pure Kotlin, with no new dependency.
