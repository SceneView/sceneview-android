- **3D Scan planes paint only what the photos saw ([#4697](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4697)).** The demo's Room Scan plane textures no longer smear the bed, the desk or other furniture onto the floor. Each key photo gets a coarse depth view, built from the dense cloud and the other planes, and a texel takes colour only from photos that saw it unobstructed. Grazing photos are weighted down or skipped. Texels no photo saw are cropped away or left neutral grey. A plane's own depth noise is not counted as an occluder: on raw depth the floor smears over about 25 cm. The bake still runs once, off the main thread, when the scan is saved.
