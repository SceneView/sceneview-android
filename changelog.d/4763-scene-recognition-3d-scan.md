- **Demo app: 3D Scan recognises doors, windows and furniture.** Replaying a scan with a dense cloud now outlines the beds, sofas, chairs, tables, cabinets, TVs and plants it shows, each with its name, size and how sure the scan is. Furniture comes from an on-device detector (YOLOX-Tiny, Apache-2.0, on LiteRT) that looks at a few keyframes; what it finds is lifted into the cloud and fused across views. Doors and windows come from the holes in the walls. Doors and windows show at once; the furniture follows once the detector is there. The detector (10 MB) is not in the APK: the first replay that needs it downloads it, checks its SHA-256 and keeps it — on mobile data only after a tap, as for the HD pack; offline, the replay runs without it and says so.
