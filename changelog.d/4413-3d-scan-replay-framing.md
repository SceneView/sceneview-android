- **`SceneView` / `ARSceneView`: replacing the environment no longer reads a destroyed skybox ([#4330](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4330)).** `rememberEnvironment(key = …)` destroys the previous environment as soon as its key changes, but the scene only received the new one a dispatch later, and Filament reads the skybox it replaces: a segfault in `Scene.setSkybox` on some swaps (a dark/light toggle that changes a colour skybox, an HDR picked from a list). The scene now lets go of an environment's skybox and indirect light before the environment is destroyed, and takes the next one in the same pass.
- **Android demo: a replayed 3D Scan sits whole inside the screen, its dimensions included ([#4306](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4306), [#4413](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4413)).** The 3D view frames the box of what the scan drew, the sizes written outside the walls included, in the part of the screen the controls leave free, in portrait and landscape. Left alone it sways gently in front of the room; a pinch, a drag or a pan can no longer put the camera inside the cloud, under the floor or far from the room. The tracking aids (trail, frusta, live points) are toned down so the room reads first.
- **Android demo: the 3D Scan replay gives the screen back to the room ([#4379](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4379)).** One thin timeline bar sits above the dock (play, scrubber, clock), and a tap on the room hides it with the header. The layers, their figures and the room's size moved into the settings sheet. A phone on its side gets its own layout, with no card over the room.
- **Android demo: 3D Scan's room size holds steady ([#4328](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4328)).** A size ARCore finds for a few frames and takes back is not shown, a smaller reading is held five seconds before it replaces a larger one, and the top bar and the dimensions on the floor always name the same room.
- **Android demo: 3D Scan shows one point count ([#4413](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4413)).** The live count included points seen only once, which a saved scan drops (32k live, 5.6k stored). The live count and the saved scan now count the same points.
- **Android demo: the Map view keeps its sizes readable ([#4413](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4413)).** Seen from above, a wall, the path or the points could hide a size written on the floor. They are now drawn over the rest of the scan.
- **Android demo: the status-bar icons stay white over a 3D or camera screen after a rotation or a theme switch ([#4413](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4413)).**
