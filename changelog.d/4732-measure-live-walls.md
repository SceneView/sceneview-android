- **Demo — Measure shows the room's walls live and says what each tap does** ([#4732](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4732)). In Wall and Room modes the walls ARCore recognises rise in place, drawn by the same live-wall layer as 3D Scan (now shared, with a world-space adapter for ARCore's plane polygons), and aiming at one lands the point on its foot with a "Wall foot" seam reticle; their ends and feet snap like plane edges. The add-point control is a single camera-style shutter button whose caption says what the next tap does (Add point, Close shape, Set height, New measure), a refused tap says why under the reticle (tracking lost, no surface, off the wall, point not pinned) with a warning haptic, and the readout numbers the measure once there are several. Length labels face the camera with the screen's up, so a floor segment seen from above no longer reads upside down.
