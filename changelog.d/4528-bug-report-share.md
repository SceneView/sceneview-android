- **"Share report" in the demo's bug report opens the share sheet on the first tap ([#4528](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4528)).** The 1,200-line app log used to ride in the share text, a ~600 KB intent that Android refused without a word. The log now travels as an attached `sceneview-bug-report.txt` through the app's existing file provider, and the share text keeps a summary of at most 16,000 characters: the note, the device and app details, and the AR tracking block.
- **The bug report keeps its AR tracking block for the whole session ([#4529](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4529)).** The demo now keeps its latest 60 AR tracking lines in memory as they are logged, instead of reading them back from a log buffer that ARCore fills in under a minute. The log buffer is only read when that history is empty.
