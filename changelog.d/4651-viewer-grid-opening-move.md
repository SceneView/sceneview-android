- **Every 3D viewer of the demo shares one ground grid and one opening move ([#4651](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4651)).** The Model Viewer, its showroom and the Explore viewer draw an endless metric grid on their floor that follows the zoom in powers of ten and fades with distance, Blender-viewport style, with no visible edge; its line colours are the new `stage-grid-minor` / `stage-grid-major` tokens, light and dark. When a model appears the camera flies onto the framing over `motion-entrance` (700 ms, expressive ease) instead of cutting to it, a double-tap zoom eases on the same curve, and the first touch takes the camera over mid-move without a jump.
