- **Live 3D map while scanning a room ([#4810](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4810)).** In the Android demo's 3D Scan, the small "Map to complete" card becomes a live 3D view of what has been captured, sharing the screen with the camera: the coloured cloud seen from above and behind the phone, heading-up, with the floor still to scan hatched in amber. A swap button gives that part of the screen back to the camera. The two parts keep their sizes while the coaching line changes, and a part being scanned again has its frame on the camera, never on the map.
