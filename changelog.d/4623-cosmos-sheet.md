- **Cosmos › Star › Spacetime: the spacetime sheet is a glowing curvature grid, not a washed grey disc ([#4623](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4623)).** The sheet's ground is now a deep slate (`cosmos-sheet`) and a Cartesian grid glows over it (`cosmos-sheet-grid`): its lines run straight on the flat plane, so every well bends them and the star's curvature reads from the default camera.
