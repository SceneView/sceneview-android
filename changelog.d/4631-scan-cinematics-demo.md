- **Demo: 3D Scan shows what is scanned and what is not, and its result presents itself ([#4631](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4631)).** While scanning, and in the replay, a heading-up coverage map in the corner keeps you fixed below its middle and turns the room under you: the floor cells the scan has (0.25 m cells) are filled, the rest of the room's estimated floor (the floor planes and the covered cells, hulled) is hatched as still to scan, the edge of what is covered is outlined and the path walked is drawn; it reads `Floor NN % covered` of that same floor. The capture's one coaching line now also says "Move slower" when the phone moves faster than depth keeps up, and where to point when a walk adds nothing for three seconds. The result screen opens on a cinematic reveal (high, wide and to the side, craning down onto the model over 2.8 s), its dock switches 3D and Plan with one continuous camera move, and a Tour pill flies the camera around the scan; every move is the new `io.github.sceneview.camera` API, and a touch takes the camera over at the speed the move had.
