- **AR sessions leave a trace of where tracking stood, and the demo reports a session that never tracks ([#4510](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4510)).** Diagnostics for the Play report in #4510 — AR samples that stay on "Getting ready" over a live camera image — whose cause is not established yet: this is not its fix, and nothing changes on screen. `ARSceneView` now writes one `AR tracking: state=… reason=…` logcat line per tracking transition (tag `SceneView`), including the camera that sits in `PAUSED` with reason `NONE`, which no `onTrackingFailureChanged` reports. A throw from the app's own `onSessionUpdated` or `onTrackingFailureChanged` is logged under that callback's name instead of as `ARCore session update failed`, and no longer skips the other callback; `onSessionFailed` / `onSessionFailure` fire exactly when they did. The Android demo files three things with its crash reports when collection is on, each once per AR session: the exception behind an AR failure it has no code for, a session that received camera frames for 10 s without ever tracking (camera state, ARCore's reason, ARCore version), and a resumed session that delivered no frame for 10 s.
