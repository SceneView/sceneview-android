- **Cinematic camera: `CameraShot`, `CameraTransition`, `CameraPath`, `CameraAnimator` and `CinematicCameraManipulator` in `io.github.sceneview.camera` ([#4631](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4631)).** A shot is an orbit around a target, so a move between two shots goes around the subject, not through it. `CameraPath.reveal(home)` is the entrance of a scan or a model, `CameraPath.tour(home)` a slow lap that loops without a seam, `animator.flyTo(home.copy(elevationDegrees = 84f))` the dollhouse-to-floor-plan move, and a fly-to another room pulls back at mid-flight so it never cuts through a wall. Every move is a function of time, lands exactly, and a touch interrupts it and hands the gesture camera the speed it had. The design behind it, for the 3D Scan live coverage and result cinematics, is in `docs/design/3d-scan-cinematics.md`.
