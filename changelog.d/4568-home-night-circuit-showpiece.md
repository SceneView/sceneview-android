- **Website: the home hero is a night circuit, rendered live ([#4568](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4568)).** The Khronos Car Concept (CC BY 4.0) sits on the wet start grid of a circuit at night — floodlit stands, pit neon, standing water, fine rain, the headlights' cones in the haze — one GLB and its own environment map, rendered by SceneView Web with lights, bloom, fog, screen-space reflections and vignette set through the public API. A 14 s one-take camera move plays once the scene is live: the gantry counts down, lights out, two flashes of the high beams, the brake lights as the camera passes the tail, a beacon on the tower, a pit sign that flickers before it holds; a drag takes over at any time, `prefers-reduced-motion` keeps the poster pose. A quality ladder (pixel budget, shadows, reflections) steps down a rung by itself when frames come in late, and starts one rung lower on a phone. The picture is a night in both themes (`.night`, DESIGN.md). The web viewer gains `setLight`, `setMaterial` / `getMaterialNames`, `setPixelBudget` and an object form of `setQuality` (`{ ambientOcclusion, antiAliasing }`).
