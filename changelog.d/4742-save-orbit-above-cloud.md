- **3D Scan's save orbit rises above the scanned room instead of flying through it ([#4742](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4742)).** When you tap Save, the view still leaves from the phone's own eye. The room's ceiling is now cut off first, the same way the saved scan's dollhouse view cuts it. The view then steps away from any wall the phone stood close to and rises straight up out of the room while tilting down. Finally it backs out over the walls into a slow top-down orbit. After that first step it never comes within 50 cm of a scanned point, and it never crosses a wall. Before, the view backed out through the wall behind the phone or up into the ceiling, and points 30 cm from the lens flashed as large white squares.
