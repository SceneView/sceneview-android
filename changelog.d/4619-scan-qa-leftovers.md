- **3D Scan: the replay's coverage map no longer says "Floor 0 % covered" under a room it shows, and Back from "View in AR" returns to your scan ([#4619](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4619)).** A scan drawn from depth now fills its coverage map from the surfaces it found, the way the 3D view draws them. Opening the AR dollhouse from a replay or a scan's model, then pressing Back, used to drop you on the list of scans; it now returns to that replay or model. The steps to stream to a computer (`adb reverse`, the `python3` recorder) never appear in the store app, and in debug builds they are folded under "Developer tools" in the settings sheet.
