- **Demo app (Android and iOS)**: searches are measured. A query typed in the Home demo filter or in Explore's online catalogs logs GA4's standard `search` event once the field has been still for 1.5 s, or when a result is opened: `search_term` (lowercased, cut to 50 characters, emails, links and long numbers replaced by `(redacted)` on the device), `surface` (`samples` or `models`) and `result_bucket`. The privacy policy lists it.
