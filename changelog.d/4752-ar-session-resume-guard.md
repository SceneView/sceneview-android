- **A camera held by another app no longer crashes an AR app on resume ([#4752](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4752)).** ARCore's `Session.resume()` throws when the camera is busy or its permission was revoked, and SceneView called it unguarded from the host's `onResume`, from a camera-permission grant and from `retrySession()`. The exception now reaches `onSessionFailed` / `onSessionFailure` (`ARCore.onArSessionFailed`) as documented, the session stays paused, and the next resume tries again. A session ARCore refused to resume no longer reads as resumed.
