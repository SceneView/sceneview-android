- **Android demo: Debug Overlay and Picking & Collision say "Scene ready" once their lighting is in ([#4448](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4448)).** Both screens named themselves ready on their first frame, while the studio lighting was still decoding: until it landed, the sphere and the floor grid were lit by the neutral fallback. The nightly render check therefore saw two pictures of Debug Overlay depending on the speed of the runner, and its reference was the unfinished one — the check went red, 3.49 % of pixels away, on the runs that showed the real screen. Both screens now wait for the lighting like the other model demos do, and the Debug Overlay reference is the finished picture.
