- **Android demo: Debug Overlay and Picking & Collision say "Scene ready" once their lighting is in ([#4448](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4448)).** Both screens named themselves ready on their first frame, while the studio lighting was still decoding: for a moment the sphere and the floor grid were lit by the neutral fallback. The nightly render check captured that moment on its faster runs and went red on Debug Overlay, 3.49 % of pixels away from the reference, three runs out of ten. Both screens now wait for the lighting like the other model demos do. The render suite also closes the demo before it ends, instead of leaving a live 3D view to be killed with the test process.
