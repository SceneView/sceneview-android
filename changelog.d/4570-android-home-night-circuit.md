- **The Android demo opens on a night circuit ([#4570](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4570)).** The home screen's hero, which flew over a low-poly desert, now plays the website's night circuit, rendered live: a concept car on a wet grid in the rain, a start gantry that counts down and goes out, headlights that flash back, brakes, neon, floodlights and a camera gliding around the car, still steered by the page's scroll. It picks its quality from the device and steps down by itself when frames run late, remembering the step for the next launch: about 60 fps on the Android Emulator and 41 fps on a Pixel 4a. New in `sceneview-core` for the same kind of light show in your own scene: `LightCues`, ready-made 0-to-1 curves (`ignition`, `fadeIn`, `breathe`, `blink`, `flash`, `glitch`) to drive a material's emission or a light's intensity from a clock.
