- **Demo app: the 3D Scan result shows the parts worth scanning again ([#4736](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4736)).** A stretch of wall never seen, floor never seen with nothing standing on it, and floor seen only sparsely are each framed in amber on the model, in 3D and in the Plan view, and counted in a pill beside the tour's. A tap lists them with their reason. Each part's "Re-scan this part" is shown but disabled until a part scanned on its own can be lined up with the first scan. Recordings opened from `.rrd` files carry no normals, so they show floor parts only.
