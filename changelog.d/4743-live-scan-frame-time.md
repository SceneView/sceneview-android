- **Demo app: 3D Scan's live screen does less work per frame ([#4743](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4743)).** The coverage map and the live walls now update only when the scene hands them something new (a new camera frame, a moved phone or a new depth snapshot). Before, they reran on every render, and the view repeats the same frame between rebuilds. The floor map's plan paper is cut once per coverage change instead of being clipped by two paths on every draw. The floor outline and the walked trail are cached, and the covered-area label recomposes only when its text changes. The map, the walls and the guidance look the same as before.
