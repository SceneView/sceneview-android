- **Website and docs: a calmer top bar and smoother scroll on the home page ([#4612](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4612)).** The home page header no longer turns into a grey glass band over the hero film. Its controls are less crowded, and the mobile menu has a title and a close button. The docs header now uses the same glass bar as the home page instead of saturated blue, shows the SceneView mark linking back to the home page, and groups its 16 tabs into 8 (every page keeps its URL). On the home page, the scroll handler does less work per frame and 3D scenes wait until scrolling stops before starting. On desktop this brings the worst frame while scrolling from 50 ms to 33 ms, and from 133 ms to 33 ms on a 4× slower CPU.
