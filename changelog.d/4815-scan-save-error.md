- **A re-scanned part that does not line up no longer loses the scan ([#4815](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4815)).** In the Android demo's 3D Scan, a part scanned again that did not line up on its scan used to fail the whole save. Try again then refused it the same way, leaving only Discard. The card now says why the part did not line up and offers "Save as a separate scan", which keeps the re-scan under its own name. Every failed save now gives its cause and the next step on screen, logs one line under the `ScanSave` tag, and offers "Save as file" when the phone cannot keep the scan. Long scans need less memory to save. The review card now shows the number of points the scan keeps.
