- **3D Scan's live points sit on the camera image ([#4716](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4716)).** While a capture follows the phone, the demo now draws the points from the phone camera's own pose and through ARCore's own projection, the one the AR view under it uses. Each point lands on the pixel it was captured from. Before, the live view stood 2.6 m behind and above the phone, with a lens of its own, over the same camera image, so a wall's points showed about a fifth of the screen below the wall. A drag or a pinch still opens the free 3D view of the cloud, and Recenter goes back to the camera's view. The camera's view has no "you are here" marker, since the phone is the viewpoint there.
