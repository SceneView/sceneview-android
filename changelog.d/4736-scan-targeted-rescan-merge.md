- **Demo app: 3D Scan can scan one part again and add it to a copy of the scan ([#4736](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4736)).** "Re-scan this part" now starts a live scan of that part. The new session is lined up on the first scan from its floor and walls, then refined point to plane, with no cloud service. Once it is lined up, the part is framed in amber on the camera and a line says how much of it is filled. On saving, the re-scan's points are added inside the part to a copy of the scan, kept as a new scan; the original stays as it was. When the two cannot be told apart for sure (another room, one flat wall, a corner that fits several places, a room that looks the same turned round), the re-scan is refused with "couldn't line up this part" and nothing is merged.
