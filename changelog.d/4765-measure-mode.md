- **Demo — Measure reads doors, windows, volume and the room plan** ([#4765](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4765)). Two opposite corners mark a door or window on a measured wall or traced room, which reads its net wall area; a traced room adds its volume. Plan draws it as 3D Scan's dimensioned plan, with PDF and text shares and a card naming each opening by wall letter, in Measure's units and the phone's decimal mark. A traced corner squares to 90° within 6° against a tracked wall, within 3° otherwise, so a skewed room keeps its shape. A wall or room corner tapped within 5 cm of the last one is refused with "Move to the next corner first" instead of adding a side of length 0. Plan stays dimmed until a room is closed and its height set, and a tap on it says what is still missing. The height and area labels on the walls now read what the card reads, in the same units, and the walls rise all the way to the height read. The hint "Aim at the wall to read its height" goes once a height has read.
