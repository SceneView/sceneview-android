- **Measure reads doors, windows, volume and the room plan ([#4765](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4765)).** In the Android demo, a measured wall or a traced room takes its doors and windows off by two opposite corners each on a wall and reads its net wall area; a traced room adds its volume to the floor area, perimeter and wall area, metric or imperial by locale. Plan opens the room as 3D Scan's dimensioned plan, with the same PDF and text shares, and a card naming each opening by the plan's wall letter. The screen is one entry point, `MeasureScreen(onBack)`, and the depth toggle and accuracy note left its settings: the depth fallback is on wherever the device has depth. Pure tests pin the snapping, wall feet, square corners and loop closing as they were, along with the snap kinds, button actions and refusals the haptics are read from, and hold the room figures to their error bounds on noisy synthetic rooms.
