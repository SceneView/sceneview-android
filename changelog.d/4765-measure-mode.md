- **Measure reads doors, windows, volume and the room plan ([#4765](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4765)).** In the Android demo, a measured wall takes its doors and windows off by two opposite corners each and reads its net area; a traced room adds its volume to the floor area, perimeter and wall area, metric or imperial by locale. Plan opens the room as 3D Scan's dimensioned plan, with the same PDF and text shares. The screen is one entry point, `MeasureScreen(onBack)`, and the depth toggle and accuracy note left its settings: the depth fallback is on wherever the device has depth. Pure tests pin the snapping, wall feet, square corners, loop closing and haptics as they were, and hold the room figures to their error bounds on noisy synthetic rooms.
