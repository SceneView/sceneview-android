- **Demo — Measure reads doors, windows, volume and the room plan** ([#4765](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4765)). Two opposite corners mark a door or window on a measured wall or traced room, which reads its net wall area; a traced room adds its volume. Plan draws it as 3D Scan's dimensioned plan, with PDF and text shares and a card naming each opening by wall letter, in Measure's units and the phone's decimal mark. A traced corner squares to 90° within 6° against a tracked wall, within 3° otherwise, so a skewed room keeps its shape.
