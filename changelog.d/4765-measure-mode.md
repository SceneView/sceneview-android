- **Measure reads doors, windows, volume and the room plan ([#4765](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4765)).** In the Android demo, a measured wall or a traced room takes its doors and windows off by two opposite corners each on a wall and reads its net wall area; a traced room adds its volume to the floor area, perimeter and wall area, in feet and inches in US English and in metres everywhere else. Plan opens the room as 3D Scan's dimensioned plan, with the same PDF and text shares, and a card naming each opening by the plan's wall letter; Measure's unit setting reaches the plan, its card and its shares, so one page reads in one unit system. The screen is one entry point, `MeasureScreen(onBack)`, and the depth toggle and accuracy note left its settings: the depth fallback is on wherever the device has depth. A traced corner squares to 90° within 6° only when ARCore tracks the next wall — a vertical plane standing on the floor, at least 1.5 m tall and 1.2 m long, square to the last wall within 3° and reaching within 20 cm of the corner, so an open door (a 0.93 m leaf included) or a cupboard does not count — and within 3° otherwise, so a room that is not square keeps its shape. An opening stays on the wall it was marked on, as wide, when the walls before it are re-measured. Pure tests pin the snapping, wall feet, square corners and loop closing, along with the snap kinds, button actions and refusals the haptics are read from, and hold the room figures to their error bounds on noisy synthetic rooms.
