- **Demo app — voice dictation keeps listening through pauses** (#4618). The bug-report note and Point & Ask's question used the system "Speak now" dialog, which Google's recognizer closes after about half a second of silence whatever the silence extras say. Both now dictate in-app through one shared session that chains recognizer segments, keeps every word heard (partials included) in the field as it goes, shows a stop button and a "Listening…" line, and ends on stop or after about 8 seconds of silence. It asks for the microphone on the first tap; refusing it falls back to the previous dialog.
