- **Demo app, 2D in 3D: one scene instead of two hidden tabs ([#4624](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4624)).** The Inspect / Media switch at the bottom was easy to miss, so half the demo went unseen. The rocket with its live Compose card now stands in front of a small gallery wall that holds the framed picture (ImageNode), the streamed clip (VideoNode), the turning badge (BillboardNode) and their labels (TextNode). Play / Pause sits in the dock; Face camera and Always on top are in the settings sheet. Old `?tab=media` links still open the demo.
- **Demo app, Animation: a real stage and smooth clip changes ([#4624](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4624)).** The fox no longer floats on a grey void under a black band: it walks on the same themed floor and sky as the other demos, in light and dark. Switching clips cross-fades for about half a second instead of snapping, and the "blend 0%" debug line is gone until you actually blend.
