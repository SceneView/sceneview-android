- **3D Scan (demo)**: the finished model is clean. Floating debris is removed, doubled surfaces are merged, and floors, walls and worktops are flattened onto their planes, including on noisy real scans where a wall was scanned twice. Small holes are closed. The model is textured from the scan's key photos, with exposure levelled and seams blended, and falls back to vertex colours where no photo sees it, and everywhere when the photos cover less than half of it. It is simplified to a phone budget, never ends up heavier than the raw surface, and finishes within a mid-range phone's memory. A large scan shows its raw surface first, then the finished model in its place. A model that failed to finish is not kept, and models saved by an earlier version are finished again. The exported and shared `.glb` is that same textured model, and any viewer opens it (#4745).
