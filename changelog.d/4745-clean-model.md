- **3D Scan (demo)**: the finished model is clean. Floating debris is removed, doubled surfaces are merged, and floors, walls and worktops are flattened onto their planes, including on noisy real scans where a wall was scanned twice. Small holes are closed. The model is textured from the scan's key photos, with exposure levelled and seams blended, and falls back to vertex colours where no photo sees it, and everywhere when the photos cover less than half of it. It is simplified to a phone budget, keeps its photo texture within an 8 MB `.glb`, and finishes within a mid-range phone's memory: a scan longer than that memory can finish is meshed with coarser cells instead. A large scan shows its raw surface first, under a "Finishing your model…" line whose dots breathe, then the finished model in its place, which the line says once (and TalkBack reads) before it fades; the line keeps its room whichever way the finish ends, so the model framed above it never moves. If finishing runs out of memory, at any stage, the raw surface stays on screen with a note saying so and is kept, so the scan reopens on it at once and finishes it again behind it, three finishes in all before the raw surface is kept for good; any other model that failed to finish says so and is not kept, and models saved by an earlier version are finished again. The exported and shared `.glb` is that same textured model, and any viewer opens it (#4745).
