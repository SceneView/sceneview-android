- **Demo: a failed in-app update now hands over to the Play Store instead of a dead-end Retry.** The update prompt is only offered when Google Play installed the app (a sideloaded or `adb`-installed build can never be updated by Play, which is what made "Update failed" appear). When the in-app flow does fail (a failed download, a failed consent modal, or a launch Play refuses), the reason and Play's error code are logged on one line (`adb logcat -s InAppUpdate`), and the prompt reads "Update failed · Open Play Store", which opens the app's store listing (the Play Store app, or the web listing without it).
