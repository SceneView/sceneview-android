- **Demo app: Room Scan is now 3D Scan, with Pause, Finish and a review before saving ([#4627](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4627)).** A clearer, more general name on every screen, the demo list and the store listing. The capture shows one compact line ("Scanning · 0:42", the points captured from zero), one coaching line, Pause and Finish. Finish opens a review where Keep scanning returns to the same capture and Save scan writes it; a failed save stays on screen with Try again, and Back during a capture asks whether to keep scanning, save or discard. Saved scans and `.svscan` files open as before.
