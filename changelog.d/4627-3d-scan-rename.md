- **Demo app: Rerun AR Replay is now 3D Scan, with Pause, Finish and a review before saving ([#4627](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4627)).** The demo listed as "Rerun AR Replay", whose start screen said "Room Scan", is called 3D Scan on Android and iOS: in the demo list, on its screens and in both store descriptions. The Android capture shows one compact line ("Scanning · 0:42", the points captured from zero), one coaching line, Pause and Finish; the clock stops while the scan is paused or tracking is lost. Finish opens a review where Keep scanning returns to the same capture and Save scan writes it. A failed build or save stays on screen with Try again, and discarding it asks first. Back during a capture pauses it and asks whether to keep scanning, save or discard. Saved scans and `.svscan` files open as before.
