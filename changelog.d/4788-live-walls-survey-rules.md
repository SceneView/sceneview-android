- **Demo app: the live walls in 3D Scan and Measure no longer show furniture as walls ([#4788](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4788)).** A vertical plane is a wall only when it stands on the floor (bottom within 0.3 m) and rises past 1.2 m, is seen long enough along one of the room's directions, and is not furniture before a wall. Two near-parallel fits of one wall are one wall, a piece fitted askew along a wall joins it, and a wall goes once no plane holds it up. Walls are drawn to the ceiling seen, else to a height measured in the same session, else to 2.50 m, never to the top of a plane.
