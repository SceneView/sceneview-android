- **Demo app: the live walls in 3D Scan and Measure no longer show furniture as walls ([#4788](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4788)).** A vertical plane is a wall only when it rises past 1.3 m and is seen over 0.4 m of its height, or over the furniture hiding the rest, as the room survey reads a wall, is seen long enough along one of the room's directions, and is not furniture before a wall. Two near-parallel fits of one wall are one wall, a piece fitted askew along a wall joins it, and a wall goes, under the same id until then, once no plane has held it up for half a second. A plane ARCore refits a few centimetres across one of these thresholds keeps its verdict, so a wall no longer flickers; a first verdict takes the 1.3 m as it is, so a cabinet just short of it is not a wall. While ARCore has lost tracking the walls stay as they are, and they keep their ids once it finds the room again, waiting for their planes as long as ARCore takes to find the floor and them; a loss is read off the camera poses, so a phone showing few frames a second still sees walls come and go. Walls are drawn to the ceiling seen (2 m up or more), else to a height measured in the same session, else to 2.50 m, never to the top of a plane.
