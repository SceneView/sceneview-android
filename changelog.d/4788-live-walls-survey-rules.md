- **Demo app: the live walls in 3D Scan and Measure no longer show furniture as walls ([#4788](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4788)).** A vertical plane is a wall only when it stands on the floor (bottom within 0.3 m), or on the furniture hiding its foot, and rises past 1.3 m as in the room survey, is seen long enough along one of the room's directions, and is not furniture before a wall. Two near-parallel fits of one wall are one wall, a piece fitted askew along a wall joins it, and a wall goes, under the same id until then, once no plane has held it up for half a second. A plane ARCore refits a few centimetres across one of these thresholds keeps its verdict, so a wall no longer flickers; a first verdict takes the 1.3 m as it is, so a cabinet just short of it is not a wall. While ARCore has lost tracking the walls stay as they are, and they keep their ids once it finds the room again. Walls are drawn to the ceiling seen (2 m up or more), else to a height measured in the same session, else to 2.50 m, never to the top of a plane.
