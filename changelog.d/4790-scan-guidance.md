- **Android 3D Scan: the floor plan export says when the room's measure is long ([#4790](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4790)).** Past its usual time, "Measuring the room…" turns into "Still measuring the room…" and the plan shows when the measure ends. A measure nobody waits for any more stops and lets the next scan be measured; only a measure that fails says so, and it is tried again the next time.
- **Android 3D Scan: steadier guidance.** The turn hint changes only once the turn has left the direction shown for three seconds. "Nothing new" no longer makes the status or the coaching line say the scan is stuck while points are still being added; a surface with no usable depth or too little texture still says so on both. The lasting "Photo limit reached" line is gone.
- **Android 3D Scan: smaller live points.** Over the camera, points close to it stay small instead of covering it. Coarse points keep their width, so walls past about a metre stay filled in the live view; nearer, gaps can show. The save flight draws points as the replay does.
