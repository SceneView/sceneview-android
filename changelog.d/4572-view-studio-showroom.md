- **Website: every model on `/view` stands in a lit photo studio ([#4572](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4572)).** The black ground gives way to a seamless studio wall that curves into the floor with no horizon line: white and grey in the light theme, a charcoal showroom with a pool of light in the dark. Each model stands on a contact shadow baked from its own geometry, over a faint blurred floor reflection, lit by a softbox studio with no sun, so car paint shows long clean highlights instead of odd reflections. Cars are framed low at three quarters through a 30-degree lens, small objects at tabletop scale, and a phone held upright gets a wider fill. Auto-rotate is a slow 15-degree-per-second turntable, and a short camera move plays on load unless reduced motion is on. The effects step down by themselves on a slow device. The web viewer gains `setStudio()` and the `create({ studio })` option, and `dispose()` releases everything again after a material lookup.
