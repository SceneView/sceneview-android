- **3D Scan ends on your model, kept on the phone ([#4633](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4633)).** In the Android demo app, Finish now opens the finished 3D model straight away: still, framed to fit the room, with its name (tap to rename), Recenter, View in AR and Export. The camera path, photos, timeline and the separate "Build 3D model" step are gone. The model is saved with the scan, so it reopens as it was, even after the app restarts. If only part of the surface can be built, you see the scan's coloured points instead. Export now leads with the 3D model as a GLB file to save or share, and the PLY, SVSCAN and RRD files are made only when you ask for them, under More formats.
