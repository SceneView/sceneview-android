- **Demo app: clearer featured carousel, a gallery that opens at once, and a way into 3D AR Model Viewer ([#4622](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4622)).** The home carousel's "Open" pill now carries an arrow, its first page is titled "Model Viewer" after the screen it opens, and Real-World Scan and Rolling Balls join Model Viewer and Cosmos as pages, drawn from their dark captures so the white copy stays readable in light mode. "Browse online models" no longer freezes the home for about two seconds on a cold start: the catalogue clients are built off the main thread, the gallery's header and a loading stage show right away, and the home cross-fades into it instead of leaving a blank beat. A loaded Sketchfab model offers one discreet action, "Open in 3D AR Model Viewer" when that app is installed, otherwise "Get 3D AR Model Viewer", which opens its Play listing.
