- **On iOS, a paused 3D Scan replay now holds its camera still ([#4325](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4325)).** Before, the turntable drift, the entrance and the drag inertia kept moving the room while the replay was paused, so a paused frame could not be read. Now the camera stops from the first paused frame, whether the pause comes from the button, a scrub or the end of a recording that does not loop. A double tap, the map view and gestures still move a paused replay, and resuming eases the drift back in. The short hold at the end of a loop is not a pause, so the camera keeps moving through it. A replay the system interrupted, for example by Control Center, a call or the app switcher, plays on when the app is back. A replay you paused stays paused.
