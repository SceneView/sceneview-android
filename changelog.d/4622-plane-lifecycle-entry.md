- **Plane Lifecycle and Surfaces: "Couldn't start AR" keeps its "Try again", on the stage ground ([#4622](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4622)).** When the ARCore session cannot start, both demos now draw the SDK's card (title, reason and an in-place "Try again") on the stage ground instead of a black viewport. Before, Plane Lifecycle showed it on black with the plane counter and a "Scanning for surfaces" banner on top, and Surfaces replaced it with a card that only said to leave the demo and reopen it. The scaffold's own card now shows only for a failure the SDK has no card for. The Plane Lifecycle counter uses the glass-over-media tokens and its labels are plural resources.
