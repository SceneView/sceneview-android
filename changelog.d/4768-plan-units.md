- **Demo app: the 3D Scan plan's units follow the phone's language — feet and inches in US English, metres everywhere else ([#4768](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4768)).** Under US English (`en-US`) the plan, its table, its PDF and its shared text give feet and inches — `13 ft 1 in`, `124 ft²`, wall labels `13' 1"`, a scale bar of round feet and a PDF line that says so. The home summary of a multi-room scan follows the same rule: its rooms table, its total, and each room's plan and figures are in square feet and feet too. Every other language, other English ones included, keeps metres.
