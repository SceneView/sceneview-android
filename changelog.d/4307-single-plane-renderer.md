<!-- category: Changed -->
- **Android AR: one plane renderer, and a wall no longer looks like a floor ([#4307](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4307)).** `ARSceneView` (and the `ARScene` alias) now always draws detected surfaces with the dots renderer that used to be the opt-in `PlaneRendererV2`, and the mark says what the surface is: round white dots on floors and tables, upright blue dashes on staggered rows on walls, hollow warm rings on ceilings. The shape differs as well as the colour, so the difference holds in a dark room, in daylight and for a colour-blind user. Every mark sits in a soft dark halo, so it stays readable on a white wall or a sunlit floor. No public API is removed: `planeRendererVersion` is still accepted and ignored, and the old names keep compiling and linking (see below).
- **Android AR: small slivers are no longer drawn as surfaces ([#4307](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4307)).** A wall under 0.40 m × 0.20 m or a horizontal surface under 0.20 m × 0.10 m is skipped until it grows, which removes the short-lived patches ARCore reports on bags, door edges and furniture fronts. A floor polygon that ARCore extends underneath an object is still drawn across it: hiding that needs depth occlusion, not a size filter.
- **Android AR: the second plane renderer and its switch are deprecated ([#4307](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4307)).** `ARSceneView(planeRendererVersion = …)` and `ARScene(planeRendererVersion = …)` are deprecated and ignored: both values draw with `PlaneRenderer`, so delete the argument. `PlaneRendererV2` and `PlaneVisualizerV2` are deprecated aliases that forward to `PlaneRenderer` and `PlaneVisualizer`; `PlaneRendererBase` and its `Version` enum are deprecated. On `PlaneRenderer`, `maxHitTestPerSecond` is deprecated for `maxUpdatesPerSecond`, `planeTexture` always returns `null`, `viewSize` is unused, and `MATERIAL_TEXTURE` / `MATERIAL_COLOR` no longer name material parameters (use `MATERIAL_GRID_TINT`). The textured-grid drawing itself and the bundled `textures/plane_renderer.png` are gone: two renderers side by side meant every AR sample picked one and the two drifted apart.

<!-- category: Fixed -->
- **Android AR: the old plane renderer held on to the materials of planes that were gone ([#4307](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4307)).** It kept the two material instances of every plane it had ever drawn until the AR view itself was destroyed, so a long session over a changing room only grew. The single renderer that remains owns each plane's instances together with its mesh and releases them when the plane goes away.
- **Demo app: the placed sofa stays movable once the surfaces fade ([#4307](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4307)).** The "Plane Renderer V2" demo is now **Surfaces**: after the first tap the surfaces fade but the sofa can still be dragged, turned with two fingers, pinched, or put elsewhere with a tap; the surfaces come back for the length of a drag; Surfaces and Reset stay in the dock; and the status line stops spinning as soon as a surface is found. The old `ar-plane-renderer-v2` link still opens it.
- **Demo app: Settings opens its sheet on every demo ([#4539](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4539)).** On a demo with no controls of its own (Surfaces, the AR View tab, AR Face, Hand Tracking, Object Labels, Orbital AR) a tap on Settings hid the dock and showed nothing: the sheet, shorter than its resting height, was sent to a detent that did not exist and stayed under the bottom edge. It now opens, with Send feedback and QA mode.

<!-- category: Tests -->
- **Android AR: JVM tests for the per-surface style, the minimum size and the after-placement rules ([#4307](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4307)).** The style and size rules are tested per `Plane.Type`; the demo's fade, drag, toggle and status-line rules live in a pure state class with its own test.
