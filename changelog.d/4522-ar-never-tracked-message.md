- **An AR session that never starts because the phone's gyroscope is silent says so ([#4522](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4522)).** A gyroscope that stops delivering samples leaves ARCore in `PAUSED` with reason `NONE` on every frame, over a live camera image: no exception, no failure callback, and a "Getting ready" card that never leaves. `arsceneview` gains `probeARMotionSensors(context)`, which listens to the gyroscope and the accelerometer for one second and returns both event counts as an `ARMotionSensorSample`, and `arMotionSensorHealth(gyroscopeEvents, accelerometerEvents, windowMillis)`, the pure verdict over them — `DELIVERING`, `SILENT` (the accelerometer delivers, the gyroscope does not) or `UNKNOWN`. The SDK draws nothing and `onSessionFailed` / `onSessionFailure` are unchanged. In the Android demo, the AR tab and AR Placement check the sensors once, ten seconds into a session that has received camera frames without ever tracking and without a reason from ARCore, and on a silent gyroscope replace "Getting ready" with a card that reads "Restart your phone" — no button, since nothing the app does brings the sensor back. A session that tracks never registers a sensor listener, and every other outcome leaves the screen as it was. The verdict leaves one `ar_session_failed` record with reason `motion_sensor_silent`, and the in-app bug report carries a `gyroscope: N events in 1 s (accelerometer: M)` row. The demo's other AR samples keep their own "Getting ready" screens for now.
