- **An AR session that never starts because the phone's gyroscope is silent says so ([#4522](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4522)).** A gyroscope that stops delivering samples leaves ARCore in `PAUSED` with reason `NONE` on every frame, over a live camera image: no exception, no failure callback, and a "Getting ready" card that never leaves. `arsceneview` gains `probeARMotionSensors(context)`, which listens to the gyroscope and the accelerometer for one second and returns both event counts as an `ARMotionSensorSample`, whose `health` is the verdict over them — `DELIVERING`, `SILENT` (the accelerometer delivers, the gyroscope does not) or `UNKNOWN`. A sensor whose listener the sensor service refuses is reported with a negative count and can only read `UNKNOWN`: a gyroscope that was not listened to is never called silent. The SDK draws nothing and `onSessionFailed` / `onSessionFailure` are unchanged. In the Android demo, the AR tab and AR Placement check the sensors ten seconds into a session that has received camera frames, in the foreground, without ever tracking and without a reason from ARCore — again ten seconds later, three times at most, when a measurement gives no verdict — and on a silent gyroscope replace "Getting ready" with a card that reads "Restart your phone" — no button, since nothing the app does brings the sensor back. A session that tracks within those ten seconds registers no sensor listener; one that starts tracking during the one-second measurement cancels it and unregisters; no listener stays registered in the background; and every other outcome leaves the screen as it was. The verdict leaves one `ar_session_failed` record per session with reason `motion_sensor_silent`, and the in-app bug report carries a `gyroscope: N events in 1 s (accelerometer: M), measured S s ago` row, dropped once a session tracks. A phone held sideways shows the card in the corner beside the mode selector, clear of the dock; at the largest text size the message is taller than the band left for it and scrolls, with a fading edge. Not covered: a phone whose accelerometer is silent too gets no verdict and keeps "Getting ready". The demo's other AR samples keep their own "Getting ready" screens for now.
