- **Android 3D Scan: the scan ranks the camera streams itself, and the bake log says what it got ([#4791](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4791)).** The default already took the back stream with the largest CPU image at 30 fps. The scan now keeps a stream that meets 1280x720 first, prefers, on the same side of 1280x720, a stream fixed at 30 fps over a larger one that may drop below it, and breaks a tie on the image with the depth sensor, then the larger GPU texture. The bake log names the streams ARCore offered, the one the scan asked for and the image size it received.
- **Android 3D Scan: the plane bake holds fewer photos at once.** Each photo is decoded on the first plane that paints from it and let go after the last one, instead of staying in memory until the scan is built. On the synthetic test room, 19 photos are held at once instead of 77.
