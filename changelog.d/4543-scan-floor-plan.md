- **android-demo**: a 3D Scan now has a floor plan, opened from its export sheet ("Floor plan · PDF · to scale, with areas and volume"). It gives the room's survey — floor area, length × width, perimeter, ceiling height, volume and the length of each wall — a rooms table with each room's area and volume and the plan's total, and a dimensioned floor plan drawn to scale, shared as an A4 PDF or as a few lines of text. The walls are measured where the scan saw them, not on the extent of what it saw: a wall seen on less than half its length is dashed and its length marked "≈", every measured figure carries the spread it was read with, and when fewer than two pairs of facing walls were seen the plan gives no area, no volume and no total, and says what to scan. Indicative plan, not a certified measurement — the screen, the PDF and the text all say so (#4540).
- **android-demo**: the floor area is read with the same shoelace as the Measure demo (`measureFloorArea`), so one outline gives one figure in both demos (#4540).
- **android-demo**: 3D Scan's live room figure (width × depth) stops at the walls. ARCore grows a floor plane past the wall it meets, and a stray floor patch under the real floor could hide the floor above it: either way the room read up to twice its area. A floor that overflows a wall is now cut at that wall, and the floor is taken where most of it was found, not at its lowest patch (#4540).
