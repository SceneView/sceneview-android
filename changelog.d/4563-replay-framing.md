- **The 3D Scan replay keeps the whole room in view ([#4563](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4563), [#4564](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4564)).** Once an orbit, a pan or its coast lets go, the camera eases its pivot and distance back onto the room's fit at the angle your finger left, so no part of the room stays under the header, the coverage card or the timeline; a pinch sets the zoom the room is held at, and a pinch in past the fit is kept as you chose it. Opening Settings draws the room smaller and higher, into the space above the sheet, and closing it brings back exactly the picture that was there before, eased both ways at the sheet's pace.
