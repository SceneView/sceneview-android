- **Website home: the hero film no longer freezes after a scroll away ([#4584](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4584) follow-up).** The 20-second limit on motion now counts only the time a stage is on screen. Before, a visitor who scrolled away 2 seconds into the night-circuit film and came back 20 seconds later found it frozen where they had left it, on a Play button. A stage that lost its 3D context and was started again no longer keeps the previous viewer in memory. A load that fails late no longer tears down a viewer the visitor started again in the meantime.
