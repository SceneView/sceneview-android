- **3D Scan keeps adding points when ARCore's depth runs late.** On a Pixel 9 a room scan stopped at 274,778 points for two minutes and said "Stuck — No depth yet" the whole time, while new walls kept appearing. ARCore was still sending depth, but each frame arrived more than 100 ms late (or with a timestamp ahead of its camera frame), and the scan threw every one away. Now, after half a second with nothing fused, the scan also takes new depth frames that arrive late, and never the same frame twice. "Stuck" now shows only when ARCore sends no depth at all. A scan that already has points then says "Depth lost" instead of "No depth yet".
