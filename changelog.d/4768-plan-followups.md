- **Demo app: a room plan traced corner by corner no longer shows a scatter it does not have, and an oblique wall is dimensioned along itself ([#4768](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4768)).** In the 3D Scan plan, a figure measured end to end (no scatter) is now written bare — `4.05 m`, not `4.05 m ± 0.05` — and the note about the scan's scatter appears only when a figure on the plan carries a `±`. Plans, tables, PDF and shared text all follow. A wall square to neither axis gets its dimension line parallel to it, outside the room, drawn at its true length, with its label beside its middle; the page leaves room for it on a side that has no other wall, and the area stays inside a room with a long leaning wall.

- **Demo app: the 3D Scan plan's units follow the phone's language — feet and inches in US English, metres everywhere else ([#4768](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4768)).** Under US English (`en-US`) the plan, its table, its PDF and its shared text give feet and inches — `13 ft 1 in`, `124 ft²`, wall labels `13' 1"`, a scale bar of round feet and a PDF line that says so. The home summary of a multi-room scan follows the same rule: its rooms table, its total, and each room's plan and figures are in square feet and feet too.
