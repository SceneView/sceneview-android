- **`sceneview-compose` on desktop moves to filament-kmp 0.8.0 ([#4706](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4706)).** The desktop viewer now runs on Filament 1.78.0 (was 1.77.3 through filament-kmp 0.7.3). filament-kmp 0.8.0 declares the skybox and image-based light as scene content instead of hoisted state, so `SceneViewer`'s desktop actual now does the same; its public API and its look are unchanged (same HDR loading, same image-based light scaled by `Lighting.ambientIntensity`, same solid or HDR background). Toggling `showSkybox` at runtime now shows the HDR background at once instead of after the next HDR reload, and keeps the image-based light at `ambientIntensity` instead of squaring it. Android, iOS and web are untouched.
