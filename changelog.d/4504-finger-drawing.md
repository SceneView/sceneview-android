- **Demo settings sheets keep their controls off the navigation bar, and Finger Drawing strokes keep their thickness to the ends ([#4504](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4504)).** At the sheet's low position, or while it is dragged, the row the window edge cut — Finger Drawing's Thickness slider — sat on the gesture bar or under the 3-button bar. The sheet's content now stops at the top of the navigation bar wherever the sheet rests, and touches in that strip go to the system, in every demo. A Finger Drawing stroke no longer thins to half its width over its last few millimetres: it keeps the thickness the finger gave it and ends in a full round cap.
