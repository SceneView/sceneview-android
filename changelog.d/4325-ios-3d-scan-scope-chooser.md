- **On iOS, 3D Scan now asks what you are scanning first, as on Android ([#4325](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4325)).** The demo is called "3D Scan" everywhere, and its landing now offers "Start scanning". That button opens "What are you scanning?" with three choices: an object, a room or an outdoor area. The wording is Android's, and the page follows the light or dark theme. The choice changes the capture the same way it does on Android. For an object, photos are taken every 8 cm or 6°, and points further than 1.5 m from the phone are dropped, as are planes outside a 3 m square round the middle of the walk. A room or an area is captured in full. The choice is saved in the recording, which is titled "Object", "Room" or "Area". An object's replay has no ground map. "A whole home" is Android only for now, because iOS has no floor plan and no room-by-room flow yet.
