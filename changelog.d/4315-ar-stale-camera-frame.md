- **Android AR: `rememberHostLifecycle()`, opt-in, keeps the camera live while an AR screen animates in and out of a `NavHost`.** `ARSceneView` follows `LocalLifecycleOwner` by default (unchanged), which inside a `NavHost` is the destination: it leaves `RESUMED` when the exit transition starts, so the camera stopped and its last image stayed frozen on screen until the transition ended. Pass `ARSceneView(lifecycle = rememberHostLifecycle())` on an AR destination with transitions: the session follows the host activity, runs while the composable is in the composition and the activity is resumed, pauses when the activity pauses, and is closed on disposal. Do not use it where the AR screen stays composed but hidden (a `ViewPager2` page, a screen under a full-screen `dialog()` destination): the camera would stay on there. When two `ARSceneView`s are resumed together, the one that resumed last takes the single camera and hands it back when it is disposed, paused, or fails to resume; on a predictive back gesture between two AR screens, the screen under the finger keeps its last frame until the gesture ends. The demo app's AR screens opt in, which fixes the frozen camera image when going back from an AR sample.
