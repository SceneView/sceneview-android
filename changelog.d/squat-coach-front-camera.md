- **Demo: Squat Coach faces you and counts out loud ([#4606](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4606) follow-up).** The demo told you to prop the phone up and squat in front of it, but ran the back camera, so the screen faced away and you could never see the count. It now opens the front camera by default (the pose is read from the camera image alone, no world tracking needed), with the selfie feed and the skeleton mirrored together, and keeps a Front / Back choice for filming someone else. Each rep is spoken ("one", "two", …) and a squat that stops short gets a "Go lower", through Android `TextToSpeech`, on by default with a Spoken count switch. Two pipeline fixes under JVM tests: a pose result that never came back no longer stalls the counter, nor frees the slot of a newer request when it finally arrives, and a restarted camera session (camera switch, looping recording) is no longer skipped until its clock passes the old one. Camera behaviour on a phone is still to be checked (`needs-device`).
