- **3D Scan's save orbit no longer flashes giant white squares when the camera passes close to the scanned cloud ([#4742](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4742)).** Each point of the dense cloud is drawn as a small square that grows as the camera nears it, so a square the camera grazed could fill the screen for a few frames. A square closer than 30 cm now shrinks towards a dot, and none is ever drawn taller than a quarter of the screen; farther than 30 cm the cloud is drawn exactly as before, with no holes in the walls. The result's floor map also reads its coverage to screen readers as a percent ("86% of the floor covered"), as on screen.
