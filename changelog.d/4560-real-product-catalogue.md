- **A catalogue of 61 real products at real size, on the website and in the Android demo ([#4560](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4560)).** The `/view` page's four sample tiles become a catalogue of everyday objects people actually own — IKEA furniture, a Tesla Model 3 and a Peugeot 2008, a Vespa and a city bike, a KitchenAid mixer, an iPhone, AirPods, a Nintendo Switch, Air Jordans and more — plus a few museum and space pieces. Each tile names the product and its real-world size, and category chips filter the list. The Android Model Viewer's Models sheet gets the same products as one shelf per category, downloaded per model from the HD pack on the card's tap rather than bundled in the APK. Every model is credited on the credits page; none is AI-generated or tagged NoAI.
