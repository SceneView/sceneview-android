- **Android demo: a snackbar no longer covers a demo's chips ([#4483](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4483)).** With the Settings sheet closed, "Demo reset" or the Record pill's "Saved" message was drawn across whatever the demo stacks above the dock — the second row of shape chips in Geometry Primitives, for instance — for as long as it stayed up. It now rests just above that stack, and rides the sheet down to that place when the sheet closes under it. On the AR placement screens it also stays above the "No surface found" card and the coaching line. A demo with nothing above its dock shows the snackbar where it always was.
