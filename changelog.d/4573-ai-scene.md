- **Android demo (debug builds only): AI Scene, a prototype where an AI rebuilds a scanned room, then furnishes it ([#4573](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4573)).** It works on the bundled ARCore session: its points, planes, keyframes and camera trail. The AI looks by casting rays from keyframes. It proposes walls, windows and objects, and a wall that no scanned point backs is refused. The code snaps what is kept onto the scan and measures it. The AI then places furniture at catalogue size and reads the paint takeoff. Every length, area and clearance comes from the code, not the model, and every step can be undone. A recorded conversation plays by default; you can also use Claude with your own API key, which is kept encrypted in the Android Keystore.
