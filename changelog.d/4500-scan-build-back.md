- **3D Scan: Back while "Saving scan…" leaves at once, and the scan is still saved ([#4500](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4500)).** Back used to do nothing until the scan was built, which can take a while on a long scan. Now the screen closes right away, the scan goes on building in the background, "Your scans" shows "Saving scan…" until it lands in the list, and a room of a whole home still joins that home's summary. If the save fails after you left, the list says so.
- **3D Scan: the list of scans no longer scrolls under the status bar and the title ([#4535](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4535)).** The landing, the "What are you scanning?" page and the whole-home summary cut their content at the bottom edge of the header, in light and dark.
