- **The end of a 3D scan asks how to finish the model ([#4630](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4630)).** Before the Android demo builds a scan's model, a sheet offers "As scanned" (the fused surface, picked first) or "Filled" (screened Poisson, fewer holes, slower). Filled says up front when the scan only fits its 40 MB at half or a quarter of the detail, and is shown but cannot be picked when the scan is too big to fill. Dismissing the sheet finishes the model as scanned; a scan that kept no surface points, or is reopened with its model kept, is not asked.
