- **Screened Poisson reconstruction for 3D scans ([#4630](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4630)).** The Android demo's scan module gains a second on-device reconstruction, in pure Kotlin. It solves a screened Poisson equation over the scan's oriented surfels on a sparse grid, then writes the result into the same TSDF that marching cubes and the GLB export already read. Small gaps between seen surfaces close; large unseen areas stay open. The model still uses the fused TSDF by default; the end-of-scan sheet that offers the choice follows.
