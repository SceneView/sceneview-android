- **Demo app: tap a piece of furniture in a scan's replay to read its size ([#4485](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/issues/4485)).** In the replay of a Room Scan, a single tap on an object lights the points it is made of, draws a box round it and writes its length, depth and height in metres next to it. A tap on another object picks that one; a tap on the floor or in the void clears it. Orbit, pan and pinch are unchanged. The box is worked out on the phone from the points the scan already stored — no model, no download: the floor is set aside, and so is a wall the scan saw from end to end; the object is grown from the tapped point, and the box is the tightest rectangle round its footprint. A region that runs into its neighbours, or anything wider than three metres, gets "Too big for one box" instead of a wrong size; a speck of depth noise under 30 cm gets no box and does not hide the furniture behind it. Known limits: the sizes are those of the part the scan saw, so a sofa scanned from one side reads short; two pieces of furniture standing against each other can come out as one box, and so can an object and the piece of wall behind it when the scan saw too little of that wall to tell it from furniture.
