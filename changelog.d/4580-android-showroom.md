- **Android demo: every model opens in a studio showroom, like the website's viewer ([#4580](https://github.com/sceneview/sceneview/pull/4580)).** In the light theme the Model Viewer is a white photo studio. In the dark theme it is a charcoal showroom with a pool of light under the subject. Both match `/view` on the website and use the same lighting maps. The floor fades into the wall with no visible line, and the subject casts a soft contact shadow. The camera height depends on the subject: low for a car, higher for furniture, tabletop height for a phone or a sneaker. Catalogue cars and phones now face the camera when they open. "Showroom" is the first choice in the Lighting sheet, and Reset brings it back. As on the website, the floor shows a faint mirror reflection of the subject's lower part; it is turned off on low-memory phones. The orbit can no longer dip below the floor's horizon.
